Official support

Let’s get you back in the game.

Help, contact information, and privacy details for FavGG—the offline mini-game arcade for iPhone and iPad.

Quick help

FavGG works offline and does not require an account. Choose a game from the arcade, then use the controls and rules shown inside that game.

If a game stops responding, close its game panel and open it again. If the issue continues, fully close and reopen FavGG.

Progress and records

Scores, favorites, streaks, settings, and game progress are stored locally on your device.

FavGG currently has no account or cloud sync, so deleting the app or clearing its data can remove those records.

Offline by design

The installed games and current level collections are bundled with FavGG. Gameplay does not depend on an internet connection.

Privacy first

FavGG contains no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, account system, chat, or user-generated content.
