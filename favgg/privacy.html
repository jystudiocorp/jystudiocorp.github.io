Privacy policy

Play stays on your device.

Effective August 13, 2026

What FavGG collects

FavGG does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information. The current app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Local game data

Scores, preferences, favorites, streaks, and game progress are stored locally on your device. They are used only to restore your games and are not sent to JY Studio or third parties.

Removing the app or clearing its data can erase these records. FavGG currently provides no account, cloud backup, or cloud-sync service.

Network access

The iOS and iPadOS editions bundle the game code and current level collections for offline play. Gameplay does not require an internet connection.

If you choose to open this support website, your browser connects to GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard request information under its own privacy terms. FavGG does not use that information for advertising or analytics.

Support email

If you email support, JY Studio receives the email address, message, and attachments you choose to provide. This information is used only to respond, investigate the issue, and maintain necessary support records. Email delivery is provided by Google.

Children and device permissions

FavGG does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else. There is no account creation, chat, user-generated content, location access, camera access, microphone access, or contacts access.

Third-party components

FavGG includes locally bundled open-source software and level data under their respective licenses. These bundled components do not add advertising, analytics, or tracking to FavGG.

Changes

If FavGG later adds accounts, multiplayer services, analytics, or other data features, this policy and the applicable store disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
